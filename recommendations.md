# Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations may help universities support responsible use of Generative AI.

## 1. Provide Clear AI Policies
Universities should clearly explain which uses of Generative AI are acceptable and which may violate academic integrity policies.

## 2. Include AI Guidance in Course Syllabi
Faculty should provide course-specific guidance explaining when and how students may use AI tools.

## 3. Teach AI Literacy
Students should receive training on:
- AI limitations
- Hallucinations
- Bias
- Fact verification
- Proper citation and disclosure
- Responsible AI use

## 4. Encourage Verification
Students should be encouraged to verify important information produced by Generative AI using reliable academic sources.

## 5. Promote Responsible AI Use
Generative AI should be positioned as a learning support tool rather than a replacement for independent thinking.

## 6. Provide Faculty Training
Faculty should receive resources and training to help them understand Generative AI and establish appropriate classroom policies.

## Future Research
Future studies could examine:
- Differences in AI use across academic disciplines
- Differences between undergraduate and graduate students
- Changes in AI usage over time
- Relationships between AI use and academic performance
- Student understanding of AI-generated misinformation
