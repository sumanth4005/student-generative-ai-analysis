# Research Questions

## RQ1
What are the primary ways university students use Generative AI tools in their academic coursework?

## RQ2
How do university students distinguish between acceptable and unacceptable uses of Generative AI in academic assignments?

## RQ3
What is the relationship between students’ awareness of institutional AI policies and their reported use of Generative AI tools?

## Purpose
These research questions are designed to explore student behavior, responsible AI use, and awareness of university policies related to Generative AI.
