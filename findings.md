# Findings
> Note: The dataset used in this portfolio project is synthetic and is intended only to demonstrate the analysis process.

## Overview
This project explores how university students use Generative AI for academic work and how they understand acceptable use and institutional AI policies.

## Key Findings

### Generative AI Usage
Students reported using Generative AI for a variety of academic activities, including brainstorming, writing support, summarization, research assistance, and learning difficult concepts.

### Common Use Cases
The most common uses of Generative AI included:
- Brainstorming ideas
- Improving writing
- Summarizing information
- Research assistance
- Learning difficult concepts

### Policy Awareness
Student awareness of university AI policies varied. Some students reported understanding institutional guidelines clearly, while others were uncertain about what types of AI use were permitted.

### Responsible AI Use
Many students recognized the importance of verifying AI-generated information before using it in academic work.

### Accuracy Concerns
Students also expressed concerns about inaccurate or misleading AI-generated information.

## Interpretation
The findings suggest that Generative AI is becoming an important academic tool, but students may benefit from clearer guidance regarding responsible and acceptable AI use.
