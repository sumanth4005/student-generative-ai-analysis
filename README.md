# Student Generative AI Analysis

## Project Overview
This project explores how university students use Generative AI tools for academic work and how they understand acceptable and responsible AI use.

## Research Areas
- Frequency of Generative AI use
- Academic use cases
- Student perceptions of acceptable AI use
- Awareness of university AI policies
- Responsible AI practices

## Research Questions
1. How do university students use Generative AI in academic coursework?
2. How do students distinguish between acceptable and unacceptable AI use?
3. What relationship exists between awareness of institutional AI policies and reported AI usage?

## Tools
- Excel
- Power BI
- Survey Analysis
- Generative AI
- Data Visualization

## Expected Outcome
The project aims to identify student AI usage patterns and provide recommendations for responsible and effective use of Generative AI in education.
